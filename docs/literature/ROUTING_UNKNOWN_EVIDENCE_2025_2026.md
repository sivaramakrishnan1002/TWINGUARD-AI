# Focused literature review: incomplete flood evidence and routing

**Search date:** 2026-10-31. **Publication window:** 2025-01-01 through 2026-10-31 (inclusive). This is a focused, reproducible screening exercise, not a systematic review or proof that no other work exists.

## Reproducible search method

The preregistered seven exact queries below were sent to OpenAlex Works (`https://api.openalex.org/works`, `search`, date filter `from_publication_date:2025-01-01,to_publication_date:2026-10-31`, first 10 ranked records) and Crossref Works (`https://api.crossref.org/works`, `query`, date filter `from-pub-date:2025-01-01,until-pub-date:2026-10-31`, first 10 relevance-ranked records). OpenAlex reported, in query order, 244, 813, 3,794, 50, 578, 1,622, and 9,742 records. Crossref reported 461,310, 517,225, 756,173, 111,049, 531,362, 1,503,665, and 920,409. Crossref totals are broad/noisy relevance counts, not counts of relevant works. The first 10 records returned from each query were screened by title and available abstract; candidate works were followed to primary publisher/proceedings pages. Thus, counts describe API responses, not a complete screened corpus.

Exact query strings:

1. `flood-aware route planning uncertain inundation road network`
2. `flood routing incomplete flood evidence unknown edge network`
3. `three-state routing network unknown edge conditional path`
4. `conditional routing unknown road closure flood verification`
5. `verification prioritization road closures flood routing information value`
6. `decision stability scenario-based evacuation routing flood`
7. `decision-focused value of information flood road network routing`

The primary-source screening retained close methodological neighbors rather than requiring authors to use the exact phrase “three-state routing.” No screened source was found to establish that A21's exact state labels or this particular Chennai OSSE is a unique contribution. The search's top-10 API screening and time window mean this is not an exhaustive novelty search.

## Novelty assessment

Flood evacuation routing under evolving hazards, uncertain or probabilistic road accessibility, scenario comparisons, shelter choices, and information/verification prioritization already exist in the screened literature. The 2026 Bayesian active-verification and road-sensing/value-of-information papers are especially close to A21's secondary question. The exact combination of (a) preserving `UNKNOWN` as separate from passable/blocked, (b) emitting a route only as conditional when it traverses unknown edges, and (c) ranking unknown edges by counts of saved conditional OD decisions may be a useful implementation/evidence-accounting choice. This search does **not** establish it as a distinct research contribution: the ranking is a simple coverage heuristic, and nearby literature already evaluates road verification by route consequences or information value.

Accordingly, A21 makes **no novelty claim**. Any potential contribution is limited to a reproducible, explicitly three-valued comparison on this project's saved simulated OSSE inputs. No observed closures, date-matched road outcomes, or independent route validation are available. Route stability is a count over the saved simulations, not a calibrated probability or a real-world frequency.

## Search limitations and exclusions

- Search API ranking, not full-text semantic indexing, controls the first-stage candidate list; Crossref results were particularly noisy.
- Only the first 10 relevance-ranked records per exact query and index were screened. Search could miss relevant terms, non-indexed proceedings, or works published after the recorded date.
- Generic flood mapping without route/decision methods and records outside the registered date window were excluded from the close-work comparison.
- Primary publisher/proceedings records were used for the works retained above. Where a publisher page was not retrievable in this environment, the cited publisher record/search-index metadata is identified as such; bibliographic details should be checked before formal publication.
- This review cannot establish absence of prior art, field usefulness, safety, or validity of A21's assumptions.
